Common Consumer Frauds

Learn about common scams and understand how fraudsters operate.

🛒

Online Shopping Fraud

Fake websites and fake products used to steal money.

💳

UPI / Payment Fraud

Fraudsters trick users into making unwanted payments.

🎣

Phishing

Fake messages and websites designed to steal information.

📞

Fake Customer Care

Fake support agents ask for personal or payment details.

🔢

OTP Scam

Scammers try to obtain OTPs to access accounts.

🎁

Fake Offers

Fraudulent discounts, prizes and offers are used to trick users.

📦

Delivery Scam

Fake delivery messages or calls are used to collect money.

💼

Fake Job Scam

Fake jobs are used to demand registration or processing fees.

👤

Impersonation Scam

Scammers pretend to be trusted people or organizations.

📱

SIM Scam

Fraudsters may try to misuse mobile SIM information.

Safety Tips

Follow these simple steps to stay safe from online fraud.

🔐

Protect Passwords

Use strong passwords and never share them with others.

🔢

Never Share OTP

Never share your OTP, PIN or password with anyone.

🔗

Check Links

Do not open suspicious links received through messages.

💳

Verify Payments

Check the receiver's details before making a payment.

📞

Verify Calls

Do not trust unknown callers asking for sensitive information.

🛒

Use Trusted Websites

Shop from trusted websites and check website addresses.

📱

Keep Apps Updated

Keep your operating system and applications updated.

🚨

Report Suspicious Activity

Report suspicious transactions or fraud as soon as possible.

Report / Get Help

If you face fraud, take quick action and contact the appropriate official service.

What Should You Do?

  • Stop communicating with the suspected fraudster.
  • Do not share OTP, PIN or passwords.
  • Contact your bank/payment service if money is involved.
  • Save transaction details and other relevant information.
  • Report the incident through the appropriate official channel.
